## Mechanisms and decisions

| Proposal | User/problem, operation and prior art | Difference/hypothesis and expected benefit | Costs/risks; minimal experiment and stop rule | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Strong ordinary process/browser checks | Maintainer facing false green; run actual stop/restart and recovery transitions. Ordinary integration testing is established practice | Make existing verification instructions effective at the missed boundary; detect the known restart and UI defects | Author/review cost, partial coverage; execute same assertions directly and through lab. Reject if healthy fails or errors masquerade as semantic failures | **Retained.** Known faults detected; ordinary checks preferred for this case |
| Generic transition-scenario engine | Maintainer repeatedly composing lifecycle assertions; reusable prepare/act/reopen/observe abstraction, related to state-machine testing | Could reduce repeated harness maintenance across domains | Extra abstraction and mappings; compare two uses against direct composition. Abandon if merely duplicating existing adapters | **Simplified** to composition of existing queue runner/adjudicator. No generic engine justified; no formal-method claim |
| Smaller context delivery | Agent/user suffering excess tokens or omitted handoffs; selectively load existing references (already prescribed) | Could improve execution/cost without another rule | May omit relevant constraints; require matched native ablation and maintenance checks; abandon if reliability drops or savings do not repeat | **Indeterminate.** Historical token difference is insufficient; current A/B/C smoke is feasibility, not an ablation trial |
| Universal autonomous orchestrator or semantic completion certificate | Broad coordination proposal | Would require trustworthy host/tool/budget and semantic evaluation contracts not provided by hashes | High implementation/adoption cost; no observed need that simpler checks fail to address here | **Not selected for this delivery.** No general controller added |
